PROLOGUE01
I

Who I Am

I am not only the person people see when I smile, make a joke, or say that everything is fine.

There is a quieter side of me too. I think deeply about the people I care about. I remember small details. I can spend a long time thinking about a decision because I want to make the right one.

I like creating things, drawing, taking photographs, making things with my hands, travelling and discovering new stories. I also love the feeling of learning something that I could not do before.

I am still becoming the person I want to be. I don't think that process ever really ends.

CHAPTER II02
II

Where I Came From

I learned a lot through work and responsibility. I started in gastronomy and spent years learning how to work with people, solve problems quickly and keep going when the day became difficult.

I went from being a waiter to taking on more responsibility, eventually working as a manager on international trains.

Those experiences changed me. They taught me patience, discipline and independence — but they also taught me how exhausting it can be to always be the person who has to keep everything moving.

CHAPTER III03
III

The Things I Don't Always Say

There are thoughts I usually keep to myself.

Sometimes I worry about the future. I wonder whether I am making the right choices, whether I will be able to build the life I imagine, and whether I am doing enough.

I can overthink small things. I can become afraid of losing someone who matters to me. I can worry that one wrong decision could change everything.

And sometimes I simply get tired — not only physically, but from carrying too many thoughts at the same time.

I don't always know how to explain those feelings. So sometimes I stay quiet.

CHAPTER IV04
IV

My Fears & My Strength

My fears do not disappear just because I hide them.

I fear disappointing people I love. I fear losing something important after finally finding it. I fear standing still while everyone else moves forward.

But I have learned that courage is not the absence of fear. Sometimes courage is simply waking up, taking a breath and continuing anyway.

CHAPTER V05
V

The Life I Built

For a long time, work took a huge part of my life.

Long shifts, trains, different cities, responsibility and very little time to simply stop taught me how much I value ordinary things: coming home, having a free evening, sleeping properly, creating something, and spending time with someone important.

Now I want to build a life that is not only about working hard. I want a life that I can actually live.

CHAPTER VI06
VI

The Person I Want To Become

I want to keep learning.

I want to improve my English so I can speak freely instead of searching for every word. I want to develop new skills, find work that gives me a better balance, and continue creating things that make me proud.

I want to become calmer, stronger and more confident — without losing the sensitive part of myself.

Most importantly, I want to build a future where I have time for the people I love, not just money or achievements.

CHAPTER VII07
VII

The Future I Imagine

I do not have every detail planned.

But I know what I want the feeling of my future to be: peaceful mornings, a place that feels like home, people I can trust, new memories, little traditions and enough time to enjoy them.

I don't need a perfect life. I want a real one.

EPILOGUE08
VIII

Then You Came

✦♥

And then, somewhere between ordinary days and unexpected conversations, you appeared in my story.

I don't want this page to retell everything that happened between us. That story already lives on the main page of this website.

This page is about something different.

It is about the fact that after everything that came before, there was suddenly someone I wanted to make space for in the future I was still trying to build.

Maybe this is where one story ends — and another begins.

CHAPTER II
THE ONES I REMEMBER

The Ones I Remember

There are people who become part of our story, even if they don't stay until the end.

Some walked beside me for years. Some were there only for a short time.

Some memories are warm. Some still hurt.

But I remember them all.

The people I lost. The people who changed me. The people who taught me something. And the people whose names I may no longer say, but whose memories still remain somewhere inside me.

I don't want to forget them.

Not because I want to live in the past, but because every person who truly touched our life leaves something behind.

A lesson. A memory. A piece of who we became.

And maybe remembering someone is another way of saying:

“You mattered.”

So this chapter is for everyone I once loved, cared about, laughed with, or simply shared a part of my life with.

I remember you.

And I carry the good things you left behind with me.

Some people leave our lives. Their memories don't have to.
CHAPTER III
THE HEART I FOUND AGAIN

The Heart I Found Again

There was a time when I kept almost everything inside.

I learned to be quiet. I learned not to trust too easily. I learned to keep people at a distance.

I had been betrayed more than once. I experienced loss. I lost people who were important to me, and some goodbyes came before I was ready for them.

Little by little, I became more closed. It was easier to protect my heart than to risk giving someone the chance to break it again.

For a long time, I thought that was simply who I was.

But somewhere along the way, something changed. I found someone I genuinely want to care for — someone I want to support, help, and be there for, not only when everything is easy, but especially when it isn't.

Being strong doesn't always mean protecting yourself from everyone. Sometimes being strong means allowing yourself to care again. To trust again. To love again. To stay.

I found someone I want to stand beside. And maybe that's how I slowly learned to open my heart again. ❤️

CHAPTER IV
THE THINGS I LOVE

The Things I Love

There are little things that have always caught my attention. Not because they are rare or expensive, but because they make the world feel a little more beautiful.

Cherry blossom petals. 🌸 I love the way they move with the wind — delicate, quiet, and almost unreal.

Flowers. Especially roses. 🌹 Maybe I like that a rose can be soft and beautiful while still having thorns.

I also love little lights in the darkness — a warm light in a quiet room, a street glowing at night, a small light that makes a dark place feel less lonely.

They remind me that even in darkness, there can always be something beautiful.

A flower. A light. A falling petal. A small moment that makes you stop and look. Maybe happiness is made from moments exactly like these.

CHAPTER V
THE NIGHT WITHIN ME

The Night Within Me

Night has always been different for me.

When the world becomes quiet and most people are asleep, I often find myself sitting alone with my thoughts.

I think about my life. About where I came from. About where I am now. And about where I want to go.

I think about my dreams — the ones I still haven't achieved. I imagine the life I want to build, the things I want to create, the places I want to see, and the people I want beside me.

But the night is not only about dreams. Sometimes my thoughts become heavier. I remember things I wish had happened differently, things I couldn't change, people I miss, moments I still carry somewhere inside me.

At night, there is nowhere to hide from yourself. In the silence, I can be completely honest with myself.

The night doesn't solve everything. But it gives me a place to understand myself — and sometimes, that's enough. 🌙

CHAPTER VI
THE MARKS I CARRY

The Marks I Carry

My tattoos are more than drawings on my skin.

My first tattoo came when I was 18. It represents change — the person I was before it is not exactly the person I am today.

I have experienced things that changed the way I think, the way I see people, and the way I see myself.

Then, at 19, I got my second tattoo. It represents something different: I see everything. The good. The bad. The beautiful. The painful.

These tattoos remind me that I cannot go back and become the person I once was. And I don't want to.

Every change leaves a mark. Mine just happen to be visible. 🖤

CHAPTER VII
NOTHING TO HIDE

Nothing to Hide

There is something I want you to know about me.

I don't hide my phone. Not because I have nothing private in my life, but because I don't want to build our relationship around secrets.

If you ever feel curious, you can ask. If you ever want to check something, you can.

I have nothing to hide from you.

Trust is not something I want to demand. It's something I want to build.

My phone is just a phone. But sometimes the little things show how much you trust the person beside you.

If one day you ask, “Can I check?”
My answer will be simple: “Of course.” ❤️

Memory Before You · A page I don't usually show

The Things I Survived

There were periods of my life when I wasn't okay.

There were moments when I felt lost inside my own thoughts. I struggled with depression, emotional pain, and things I didn't always know how to explain.

Sometimes I looked completely fine from the outside. I could talk, smile, work, joke, and continue my day.

But inside, things could feel very different.

There were days when simply getting through the day already felt like enough.

I learned what it was like to carry things silently. I learned how exhausting it can be when your own mind becomes a place you don't want to stay in.

But I learned something else.

A difficult period doesn't have to become the definition of your entire life.

I didn't become stronger because everything suddenly became easy. I became stronger because, little by little, I kept going.

I learned to understand myself. I learned that asking for help isn't weakness. I learned that healing isn't always a straight line.

Some things still hurt. Some memories still return. But they don't have to decide who I become.

I survived versions of myself that I once thought I would never escape.

And if you're going through a difficult chapter of your own right now, I don't want you to think you have to become okay overnight.

You don't.

Take one day at a time. You are allowed to be tired. You are allowed to struggle. And you are still worthy of kindness while you're finding your way through it.

I can't live your difficult days for you.

But if you ever need someone beside you while you walk through them...

I'm here. ❤️

This page doesn't ask you to be strong. It only asks you to keep going.
