The story of Oksy
There are parts of a person you cannot see from the outside.
PROLOGUE01
I

Who I Am

I am not only the person people see when I smile, make a joke, or say that everything is fine.

There is a quieter side of me too. I think deeply about the people I care about. I remember small details. I can spend a long time thinking about a decision because I want to make the right one.

I like creating things, drawing, taking photographs, making things with my hands, travelling and discovering new stories. I also love the feeling of learning something that I could not do before.

I am still becoming the person I want to be. I don't think that process ever really ends.

CHAPTER II02
II

Where I Came From

I learned a lot through work and responsibility. I started in gastronomy and spent years learning how to work with people, solve problems quickly and keep going when the day became difficult.

I went from being a waiter to taking on more responsibility, eventually working as a manager on international trains.

Those experiences changed me. They taught me patience, discipline and independence — but they also taught me how exhausting it can be to always be the person who has to keep everything moving.

CHAPTER III03
III

The Things I Don't Always Say

There are thoughts I usually keep to myself.

Sometimes I worry about the future. I wonder whether I am making the right choices, whether I will be able to build the life I imagine, and whether I am doing enough.

I can overthink small things. I can become afraid of losing someone who matters to me. I can worry that one wrong decision could change everything.

And sometimes I simply get tired — not only physically, but from carrying too many thoughts at the same time.

I don't always know how to explain those feelings. So sometimes I stay quiet.

CHAPTER IV04
IV

My Fears & My Strength

My fears do not disappear just because I hide them.

I fear disappointing people I love. I fear losing something important after finally finding it. I fear standing still while everyone else moves forward.

But I have learned that courage is not the absence of fear. Sometimes courage is simply waking up, taking a breath and continuing anyway.

CHAPTER V05
V

The Life I Built

For a long time, work took a huge part of my life.

Long shifts, trains, different cities, responsibility and very little time to simply stop taught me how much I value ordinary things: coming home, having a free evening, sleeping properly, creating something, and spending time with someone important.

Now I want to build a life that is not only about working hard. I want a life that I can actually live.

CHAPTER VI06
VI

The Person I Want To Become

I want to keep learning.

I want to improve my English so I can speak freely instead of searching for every word. I want to develop new skills, find work that gives me a better balance, and continue creating things that make me proud.

I want to become calmer, stronger and more confident — without losing the sensitive part of myself.

Most importantly, I want to build a future where I have time for the people I love, not just money or achievements.

CHAPTER VII07
VII

The Future I Imagine

I do not have every detail planned.

But I know what I want the feeling of my future to be: peaceful mornings, a place that feels like home, people I can trust, new memories, little traditions and enough time to enjoy them.

I don't need a perfect life. I want a real one.

EPILOGUE08
VIII

Then You Came

✦♥

And then, somewhere between ordinary days and unexpected conversations, you appeared in my story.

I don't want this page to retell everything that happened between us. That story already lives on the main page of this website.

This page is about something different.

It is about the fact that after everything that came before, there was suddenly someone I wanted to make space for in the future I was still trying to build.

Maybe this is where one story ends — and another begins.
