████████ · FINAL NIGHTMARE

THE BIGGEST NIGHTMARE

The white girl runs. He has to follow her. If he fails, the ground disappears and the abyss waits below — with the sword from the first nightmare.

WHY SHE RUNS
She is not simply a monster. She represents the memory he keeps trying to reach.

THE LAST RUN
When he finally catches her, she says: “You don't have to run anymore.” The abyss disappears. The sword falls. He does not run from what he remembers.

AFTER THE NIGHTMARE
He is no longer afraid of the dream. Fear may still exist, but it no longer controls him.

WHAT HE FEARS NOW
Not the sword. Not the abyss. Not the girl. His greatest fear is becoming the person he would have been if he had stopped walking forward.
